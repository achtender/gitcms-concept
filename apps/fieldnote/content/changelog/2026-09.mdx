**Added**

- A shared `@repo/ui` package with Supabase-inspired tokens and typography.
- Author profiles with linked journal entries.
- An internal handbook with ordered sections.
- RSS, sitemap, and robots routes.
- Draft filtering in every collection loader.

**Changed**

- Journal entries are sorted by date in the loader instead of each page.
- Site settings, navigation, and homepage copy moved into `content/site`.
