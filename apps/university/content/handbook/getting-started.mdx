Welcome to Northbridge. This chapter is the short version of everything that will make more sense once you are here.

## Before you arrive

- Activate your student account and check the email address it forwards to.
- Complete your module registration before the deadline in the calendar.
- Upload a photo for your student card.
- Tell us about any access requirements, even if you are not sure they count.

## Your first week

Orientation is on the calendar. Attendance is not compulsory, but the practical sessions on IT, the library, and student support save a surprising amount of time later.

## Who to ask

| Question | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Module choice | Your programme adviser |
| Timetable or room change | The teaching office |
| Health, wellbeing, or access | Student support |
| Lost card or account access | The help desk |

<Callout variant="note">
  If you are not sure who to ask, start with student support. They will either
  help or point you to the right person, and they will not mind the question.
</Callout>
