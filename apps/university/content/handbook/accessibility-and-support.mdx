Support at Northbridge is not a separate building you visit once. It is a set of people and adjustments that should be available throughout the year.

## Academic adjustments

If you need extra time, a different format, or a quieter room, contact student support as early as you can. Adjustments can usually be arranged without disclosing a diagnosis to individual tutors.

## Wellbeing

The counselling service offers short-term support and referrals. Appointments are free and confidential. The student union also runs peer listening sessions during term.

## Practical help

- Assistive technology and loan equipment from the library
- Accessible transport information from the estates office
- Emergency financial support from the student services team
- Quiet rooms in the library and the union building

## Ask early

The most common piece of advice from students who have used these services is the same: ask earlier than you think you need to. Support works best before a deadline has already passed.

<Callout variant="note">
  You do not need a formal diagnosis to ask for help. A conversation is a valid
  starting point.
</Callout>
