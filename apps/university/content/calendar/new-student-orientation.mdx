Orientation is designed to answer the questions that do not fit in an email. The day mixes short presentations with small-group time and a walk across every part of campus.

## The day at a glance

- Welcome from the vice-chancellor
- Programme meetings with academic staff
- Library, IT, and student support sessions
- Lunch with student ambassadors
- Optional campus walk at 15:00
