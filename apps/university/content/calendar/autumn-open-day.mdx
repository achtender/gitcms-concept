Open days are deliberately unhurried. The day begins in the Great Hall with a short welcome from the admissions team, then splits into campus tours led by current students.

## What to expect

- A sample seminar in the humanities and sciences
- Drop-in sessions with faculty and student support
- Lunch in the dining hall, with time to talk to current students
- A closing question-and-answer session with the admissions team

No registration is required for the tours, but booking a place helps us plan lunch.
