Bring a paragraph from a paper, essay, or project description that is not quite working. We will read a few together, revise on the spot, and leave with a checklist for the next draft.

## Before you come

- Bring one page of writing, printed or on a laptop
- No preparation beyond that is required
- Places are limited to eighteen students
