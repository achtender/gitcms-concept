Professor Elena Marsh traces a decade of walking through mid-sized cities and asks what makes an ordinary street feel cared for.

## About the lecture

The talk draws on fieldwork from the urban studies programme and is followed by a reception in the north foyer. Seats are not ticketed, but the hall fills early.
