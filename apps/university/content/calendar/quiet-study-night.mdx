The library keeps its doors open until midnight during the final week of term. Silent study is on the upper floors; the ground floor stays available for group work.

## What is available

- Extended opening hours
- Extra power strips and desk lamps
- Tea and coffee in the foyer from 20:00
- Librarians on call for reference questions
