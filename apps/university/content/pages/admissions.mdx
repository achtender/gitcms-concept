Northbridge admits students who are curious, prepared to work in small groups, and willing to be surprised by what they find here. We read every application as a whole rather than filtering on a single number.

## What we ask for

- A completed application form
- A short piece of recent written work
- Two references, at least one academic
- A statement about why this programme, in your own words

## Dates

The campus calendar carries the open days, application deadlines, and registration windows. If a date is not there, it is not confirmed.

## Visits

Open days are listed on the [calendar](/calendar). If you cannot make one, contact the admissions office and we will arrange a conversation with a current student.

<Callout variant="note">
  There is no advantage to applying before you are ready. A considered application
  is more useful than an early one.
</Callout>
