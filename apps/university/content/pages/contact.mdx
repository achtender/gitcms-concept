## Visiting

The campus is a ten-minute walk from Northbridge station. The main gate is open from 07:00 to 22:00 on weekdays, and the library keeps its own hours, published on the [calendar](/calendar).

<Callout variant="note">
  For urgent mental health support, contact the emergency number on the student
  support page rather than the general enquiries address.
</Callout>
