At ten in the evening the library changes character. The group tables empty, the photocopier stops, and the building becomes a place for people who have decided to stay.

## The questions change

During the day, students ask where to find things. After dark, they ask whether they are doing the assignment right. That is not a reference question, but it is the one that matters at that hour.

We keep two librarians on the late shift, not to patrol the silence but to be findable. A person at a desk is a different kind of resource from a search box.

## What the late shift needs

- A clear closing time, published on the calendar and at the door
- Tea and coffee that do not require leaving the building
- A quiet room that is actually quiet
- Permission to ask for help without an appointment

## A small ritual

At 23:30 the night librarian walks the floors and tells anyone still working that the building closes in half an hour. It is not a warning. It is a chance to pack up without panic.

The students who stay latest are rarely the ones who are behind. They are the ones who have found a question they want to finish. The least we can do is leave the lights on.
