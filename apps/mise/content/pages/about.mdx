Mise means *put in place*: the work of setting a table, arranging a counter, and making a room feel ready before anyone arrives. It is also a small café with two locations in Portland.

## The kitchen

We bake in the Downtown kitchen before either room opens. Pastry goes to the Harbour counter by bicycle, which is slower than a van and faster than you would think.

Our menu is short on purpose. Coffee, a handful of pastries, two or three things for lunch. It changes with the market and with the season.

## The rooms

Downtown has the long counter and the back room. Harbour has a window, a bench, and no lunch service. Both are for sitting, not just for passing through.

## The coffee

We work with a roaster two neighbourhoods away. The espresso blend stays consistent; the filter changes whenever a new lot arrives. Ask what is on the bar.
