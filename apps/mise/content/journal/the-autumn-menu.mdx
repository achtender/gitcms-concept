Every season we change about a third of the menu. Enough to notice, not enough to make anyone relearn how to order.

## What is arriving

- Roasted squash with brown butter and sage.
- A new sandwich: smoked cheddar, apple, and mustard on rye.
- Pear and almond cake, which replaces the plum cake for a few weeks.
- A warm spiced drink that we are still arguing about.

## What is leaving

The tomato tart and the cold noodle salad. Both will be back next summer. The cardamom bun is not going anywhere; please stop asking.

<Callout variant="note">
  Menu changes are published here and on the board by the door. If you are
  planning a visit for something specific, send us a message first.
</Callout>

## How we decide

We cook what the market has, then remove anything that is not selling. The best dishes survive both tests. The autumn menu is usually the one we look forward to most, because the kitchen slows down and the oven does more of the work.
