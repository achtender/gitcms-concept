The back room has been storage for two years. Last month we finally cleared it, knocked through the wall, and built a long oak table that seats fourteen.

## Why a back room

We wanted a room that could hold a meeting, a reading, or a very slow lunch without asking the rest of the café to be quiet. The front room is for coming and going. The back room is for staying.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Bookings">
  The back room is available for groups of eight or more. Email us at
  hello@mise.8ndr.de and tell us what you need.
</Callout>

## What changed

- A long shared table, built by a furniture maker two streets away.
- Better light, including two lamps that are not quite centred. We are told this is charming.
- A shelf for books and newspapers. Take one, leave one.
- A second door, so the room can be opened in the evening without moving the coffee bar.

## What did not change

The menu. The room is for sitting longer, not for eating differently. The same pastries, the same lunch, the same coffee.

We are open from Thursday. Come and use it.
